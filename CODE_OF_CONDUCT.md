# Code of Conduct

Commander welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone
participating in the project, including maintainers, is expected to help keep
the community respectful and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and welcoming to newcomers.
- Discuss ideas and code on their merits; give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect different perspectives, identities, and levels of experience.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and correct them.
- Respect privacy and consent when sharing information about others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, repeatedly targeting people, or continuing
  unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Retaliating against someone who raises a concern or participates in a review.

Good-faith disagreement and criticism of the project are welcome. Keep criticism
focused on the work and its impact.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
community spaces. It also applies when someone represents Commander publicly,
such as through an official account or at an event.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to [security@defos.ai](mailto:security@defos.ai), using
the subject **Commander Code of Conduct**. Include what happened, where and when
it happened, and any relevant links or evidence. Share only the personal
information needed to understand the report; do not post sensitive details in a
public issue.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers review reports fairly and share information only with people who
need it to assess or address the concern. A maintainer involved in a report must
step back from its review.

Depending on severity and repeated behavior, maintainers may request a correction,
remove content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Serious incidents may result in an immediate ban. When practical,
the person affected will receive the reason and scope of the decision privately.

Anyone affected by a decision may request reconsideration through the same private
reporting channel. A maintainer who was not involved in the original decision
should review the request where possible.
